# Review deck acceptance checklist

## Scientific content

- The scope and key terms are defined.
- Each slide has one main claim or communication job.
- Direct measurements, response markers, and inferred mechanisms are distinguished.
- Published cases state sample, perturbation, readout, and interpretation boundary.
- Method limitations and required controls are visible, not hidden only in notes.
- Recommendations follow from the evidence presented.
- Domain-specific controls and evidence standards have been applied where relevant.

## Sources and figures

- Every external figure is traceable to the original paper or authoritative source.
- Every non-trivial external claim has a source in the same slide's notes.
- Generated diagrams are labeled conceptual and cannot be mistaken for data.
- Experimental images have not been synthesized or cosmetically altered beyond transparent crop, scale, and legibility adjustments.
- Figure captions explain why the panel is shown.

## Language

- Titles are concise and sound natural in a biological report.
- Chinese wording is rigorous, plain, and free of promotional or formulaic AI phrasing.
- Uncommon abbreviations are expanded at first talk-track occurrence on the slide and in notes.
- Claims use calibrated verbs such as “显示”“提示”“支持”.

## Visual design

- The reference template's master, layouts, margins, title line, footer, and page numbers are preserved.
- Background, font family, colors, and heading hierarchy are consistent.
- Multiline text uses 1.2–1.5 line spacing.
- Body text and figure labels remain legible at full-slide view.
- No unintended overlap, clipping, stranded punctuation, or awkward one-word wrap remains.
- Literature figures are cropped to relevant panels and retain necessary labels.

## Notes and delivery

- Every slide contains `【讲稿】` and `[Sources]`.
- Notes can be delivered naturally within the intended total duration.
- Main-slide notes contain `【建议时长】`; their total is compatible with the requested duration.
- Backup slides, when requested, follow the main summary, contain `【备用页】`, and are excluded from the main duration.
- The main talk remains understandable without opening any backup page.
- The final file is PPTX and opens without repair warnings.
- Every slide has been rendered and individually inspected.
- A final automated overflow check and the applicable duration or page-count audit pass: `--target-minutes N`, `--target-main-slides N`, or both; use `--require-backup` when applicable.
