# Chinese scientific language and terminology

## Write for a supervisor-facing lab meeting

Use concise subject–predicate sentences and a restrained, evidence-led tone. Organize explanations as question → experiment → observation → interpretation → boundary. Borrow the clarity and biological framing of high-quality Chinese life-science reporting, but avoid news-style hype.

Prefer:

- `结果显示` for direct measurements;
- `结果提示` for preliminary or indirect evidence;
- `这些数据支持` for a conclusion consistent with several observations;
- `尚不能证明` for an unsupported causal or mechanistic claim;
- `需要结合……进一步验证` for a defined evidence gap.

Avoid:

- `首次揭示`, `全面阐明`, `颠覆性`, `革命性`, `强力赋能`, and `至关重要` without a precise reason;
- vague `研究表明` without identifying the experiment or source;
- promotional titles, decorative rhetorical questions, repetitive three-part slogans, and filler transitions;
- using `显著` when no statistical test supports it;
- long noun chains, excessive passive voice, and article-abstract prose on slides.

## Remove formulaic AI phrasing

When `Humanizer Zh` is unavailable:

1. Delete empty framing such as `值得注意的是`, `从某种意义上说`, and `总体而言`.
2. Name the biological object, perturbation, measurement, and comparison.
3. Replace symmetric bullet templates with the actual evidence order.
4. Vary sentence length naturally while keeping one claim per sentence.
5. Replace absolute verbs with calibrated evidence verbs.
6. Remove conclusions that merely restate the slide title.
7. Read notes aloud and rewrite sentences that sound like an abstract rather than oral explanation.

## Write titles and conclusions

- Supported conclusion: `处理X降低了条件Y下的信号Z`.
- Exploratory question: `处理X是否改变Y定位？`.
- Incomplete evidence: `处理X伴随Y信号下降`.
- Keep `一句话结论` to one sentence, include the tested context, and do not exceed the evidence.

## Expand terms

At first spoken occurrence, write on the slide and in notes:

```text
中文名称（English full name，ABBR）
```

Place it in genuine blank space, a restrained footer, or a method annotation. Do not cover data or compete with page numbers. Do not invent an expansion for a published probe or construct name.

Default exemptions: Western blot, FACS, PCR, DNA, and RNA. Audience familiarity may justify additional exemptions.

## Write speaker notes

For each result slide: state the question; name decisive conditions and controls; direct attention to the relevant panel; state the direct observation; give the justified conclusion and boundary; transition to the next experiment. Do not duplicate slide copy.
