# Results deck acceptance checklist

## Scientific content

- Every result slide visibly contains 实验目的, 实验方法, 实验结果, and 一句话结论.
- Methods show the conditions and controls needed to interpret the result.
- Direct observations, associations, functional support, and causality remain distinct.
- Biological replicates, technical repeats, statistical units, and uncertainty are not conflated.
- Negative results, limitations, batch effects, and missing controls remain visible when material.
- Every future experiment addresses a stated uncertainty and includes a decision criterion.

## Confidentiality and provenance

- No confidential term, image, condition, conclusion, filename, or derivative was sent to an external service.
- Aliases and redactions remain unchanged.
- User result pages cite `用户提供的未公开数据`; public background claims and assets have traceable sources.
- The final file contains no unnecessary comments, embedded files, external local/network links, or unused confidential media.
- The source file was preserved and the result exported as a new PPTX.

## Placeholders

- `不清楚` appears as `待确认` and its interpretation limit is stated.
- `已隐藏` appears as a correctly sized dashed `待补` placeholder.
- Image placeholders reserve enough space for the intended evidence and do not resemble synthetic data.
- No generic TODO, lorem ipsum, click-to-add, or accidental template sample copy remains.

## Language and terminology

- Chinese is rigorous, clear, direct, and free of promotional or formulaic AI wording.
- Titles and one-sentence conclusions match the evidence level.
- Uncommon abbreviations are expanded at first spoken occurrence on the slide and in notes.
- Western blot, FACS, PCR, DNA, and RNA are not expanded unless requested.

## Visual design

- Every slide has a white background and the deck uses one sans-serif family, default Microsoft YaHei.
- Clinical blue is an accent rather than a dominant fill.
- Multiline text uses 1.2–1.5 line spacing, default 1.25.
- No unintended overlap, clipping, broken image, awkward one-word wrap, or unreadably small label remains.
- Result layouts follow the evidence rather than forcing four equal cards.

## Notes and delivery

- Every slide contains `【页面类型】`, `【讲稿】`, `【建议时长】`, and `[Sources]` in notes.
- Result notes explain question, conditions, observation, interpretation, limitation, and transition.
- Main-slide timing matches the requested duration or page-count constraint.
- Every slide was rendered and inspected individually; the overflow test and strict audit pass.
- The final PPTX opens without a repair warning, and no scratch artifacts are delivered.
